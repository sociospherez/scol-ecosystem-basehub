Non-Disclosure Agreement

Solutions Callout Ltd & Dynamics Plus Max IT Solutions

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between Solutions Callout Ltd (“Disclosing Party”) and Dynamics Plus Max IT Solutions (“Receiving Party”).

The purpose of this Agreement is to protect confidential business, operational, technical, financial, customer, commercial, and strategic information exchanged between both parties in relation to ongoing and future business discussions, project engagements, partnerships, implementations, integrations, operational processes, and related activities.

Confidential Information

Confidential information includes but is not limited to:

  • Business and operational processes
  • Technical documentation and solution designs
  • Customer and supplier information
  • Pricing, proposals, and commercial terms
  • Implementation approaches and workflows
  • Software architecture and integrations
  • Internal methodologies and strategic plans

Obligations

The Receiving Party agrees not to disclose, distribute, reproduce, or misuse any confidential information without prior written consent from the Disclosing Party.

Both parties acknowledge that confidential information shall only be used for legitimate business evaluation, implementation, collaboration, or operational purposes.

Electronic Acceptance

Electronic signatures and acknowledgements submitted through this portal shall constitute valid acceptance of this Agreement.

This Agreement shall remain effective for a period of three (3) years from the date of electronic acceptance.
