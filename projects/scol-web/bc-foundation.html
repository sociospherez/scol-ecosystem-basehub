Business Central Foundation, Instance Enablement & AMC Support

A structured engagement model for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central that combines initial foundation setup, per-instance enablement, integration readiness, controlled change governance and ongoing support through an AMC model.

Engagement intent

The purpose of this proposal is to establish a practical, governed and supportable Business Central operating foundation. The focus is not limited to system configuration; it also includes stakeholder alignment, data readiness, workflow understanding, documentation, transition support and clear decision ownership.

Each Business Central instance is treated as a controlled delivery unit, with scope, assumptions, commercials and support boundaries agreed before implementation activity begins.

Instance-based commercial model
Controlled change request process
AMC support after go-live
Per instance clarity

Commercials are aligned to each BC instance/environment so delivery effort, configuration, data activity and support expectations remain measurable.

Governed delivery

Discovery, configuration, testing, approvals and change requests are managed through a clear delivery rhythm and decision log.

Ongoing support

AMC support provides continuity after go-live, covering issue triage, minor guidance, release awareness and operational improvement planning.

Commercial framework

The following structure is designed to support a phased engagement where the initial scope is clearly agreed, and any additional work is assessed through a controlled approval route.

Service area Commercial basis Typical coverage Notes
Initial BC Foundation Setup Per BC instance Core setup, configuration alignment, user/security approach, initial data readiness and operational handover. Priced per instance to avoid ambiguity where multiple companies/environments are involved.
Additional Entity / Company Per entity / company Additional setup for legal entities, posting groups, dimensions, users and relevant configuration. Scoped separately where entity structure or statutory requirements differ.
Integration Enablement Per integration stream Data flow mapping, field ownership, validation rules, import/export approach, test cycle and error handling model. Third-party connector/licensing costs are excluded unless explicitly stated.
Power BI / Reporting Pack Per dashboard group KPI definition, dataset alignment, dashboard prototype and handover guidance. Advanced data modelling or non-BC data consolidation may require separate discovery.
AMC Support Monthly retainer Remote support, issue triage, user guidance, governance check-ins and minor continuous improvement advisory. Support tier and response expectations to be agreed before contract signature.
Change Requests / Customisation Approved CR / scoped estimate New requirements, process redesign, custom workflow, additional configuration or specialist discovery after kickoff. No CR work proceeds without impact assessment and written approval.

Delivery workplan and indicative effort

Stage Indicative timing Activities Output
Kickoff & access readiness Days 1–3 Stakeholder intro, access check, instance/environment confirmation, licensing assumptions and initial delivery calendar. Kickoff pack, agreed contact route, working access and initial risk log.
Discovery & configuration blueprint Week 1–2 Process review, configuration assumptions, master data, dimensions, users, workflows and integration touchpoints. Signed scope baseline, configuration blueprint and implementation backlog.
Foundation configuration Week 2–5 Core BC setup, company configuration, roles, data templates, posting setup, validation cycles and user walkthroughs. Configured instance ready for structured testing and data loading.
Data upload & validation Week 3–6 Template preparation, data import, sample reconciliation, exception tracking and user review. Validated opening data position and issue/exception register.
UAT, training & handover Week 5–8 User testing support, process notes, issue triage, admin guidance, operational handover and readiness review. Go-live readiness checklist, support runbook and agreed hypercare route.
Hypercare & AMC transition Post go-live Stabilisation support, issue review, improvement backlog and monthly support cadence setup. AMC support model activated with agreed service boundaries.

Any configuration, customisation, integration change or full discovery requirement identified after kickoff will be reviewed as a change request. Work will only proceed once the impact, cost and delivery timeline are agreed in writing.

Commercial assumptions
  • Fees exclude Microsoft licensing, third-party connector costs and external vendor charges.
  • Pricing is based on agreed instance/entity scope and confirmed deliverables.
  • Client-side availability, data readiness and timely approvals are required to maintain delivery timelines.
  • Formal commercials will be finalised before agreement signature.
Travel and onsite engagement
  • Remote delivery is assumed unless onsite support is explicitly agreed.
  • Travel, accommodation and related expenses are charged separately at cost.
  • Any onsite workshop or travel requirement will be quoted and approved in advance.
  • Emergency or priority onsite requests may require separate scheduling and commercial approval.
