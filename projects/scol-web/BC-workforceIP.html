Connected Workforce Intelligence Platform

Powered by Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central ecosystem integration, Power Platform, Power BI and Copilot-enabled operational intelligence.

Executive summary

The Connected Workforce Intelligence Platform creates a governed workforce operations ecosystem that brings together employee lifecycle management, HR workflows, training, certifications, approvals, documentation, workforce reporting and AI-assisted support.

Business Central provides the operational and financial backbone, while Power Platform extends the workflow layer, Power BI delivers real-time workforce visibility, and Copilot supports productivity, summarisation and operational decision-making.

Connected HR and workforce operations

The challenge

Workforce processes often sit across spreadsheets, emails, disconnected HR tools, manual approvals and fragmented records, limiting visibility and slowing operational decisions.

The approach

Use the Microsoft ecosystem to connect HR, operations, finance, document management, approvals, training and reporting into one governed workforce operating layer.

The outcome

A scalable workforce intelligence platform supporting employee experience, compliance readiness, operational transparency and continuous improvement.
