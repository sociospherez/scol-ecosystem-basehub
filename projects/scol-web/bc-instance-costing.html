Business Central Foundation, Implementation Cycle & AMC Support

A structured engagement and commercial model for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, built around a repeatable customer implementation cycle, agreed per-instance pricing, controlled change governance and ongoing support through an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC).

Engagement intent

The purpose of this proposal is to establish a practical, governed and supportable Business Central operating foundation. The focus is not limited to system configuration; it also includes stakeholder alignment, data readiness, workflow understanding, documentation, transition support and clear decision ownership.

Each customer implementation or Business Central instance is treated as a controlled delivery cycle, with scope, assumptions, commercials and support boundaries agreed before kickoff. The standard foundation implementation fee is AED 30,000 per customer implementation cycle, with AMC calculated at 20% of the agreed implementation cost.

Commercial assumptions
  • Fees exclude Microsoft licensing, third-party connector costs and external vendor charges.
  • The standard BC customer implementation / kickoff cycle is priced at AED 30,000 unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • AMC is calculated at 20% of the total agreed implementation cost and activated after go-live / handover.
  • Pricing is based on agreed customer implementation scope, instance/entity assumptions and confirmed deliverables.
  • Client-side availability, data readiness and timely approvals are required to maintain delivery timelines.
  • Formal commercials will be finalised before agreement signature.
Travel and onsite engagement
  • Remote delivery is assumed unless onsite support is explicitly agreed.
  • Travel, accommodation and related expenses are charged separately at cost.
  • Any onsite workshop or travel requirement will be quoted and approved in advance.
  • Emergency or priority onsite requests may require separate scheduling and commercial approval.
