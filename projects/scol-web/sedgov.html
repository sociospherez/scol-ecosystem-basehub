Start: The Story

SedsConnective’s mission is to support neurodivergent people. IT helps by being inclusive, safe, and sustainable. The Trustees steer strategy, the IT Lead runs daily operations, and volunteers make it work — with everyone’s feedback shaping improvements.

Who decides?

  • Trustees: Strategy, big spend, major systems.
  • IT Lead: Access, tools, onboarding, change control.
  • Volunteers & Staff: Input, delivery, feedback.

Reviews

  • Quarterly governance review (Trustees + IT Lead).
  • Annual audit of data, access, and backups.
  • Decision Log keeps changes transparent.

Pillar A – Sustainability

Our IT must be financially viable, environmentally conscious, and operationally resilient.

Policy Commitments

  • Financial: Cloud‑first (Microsoft 365), maximise charity discounts, avoid duplicate tools, annual spend review.
  • Environmental: Prefer energy‑efficient cloud, refurbish & reuse devices, digital‑first workflows, responsible suppliers.
  • Operational: Document processes and roles, maintain a knowledge base, annual roadmap reviews, test continuity plans.

KPIs

  • Predictable costs (≤10% annual variance)
  • No redundant licenses/apps
  • 100% roles have handover notes
  • Evidence of device reuse & reduced printing

Pillar B – Data Treatment & Ethics

We handle data lawfully, ethically, and transparently to protect privacy and trust.

Policy Commitments

  • Compliance: GDPR & UK DPA; Trustees are Data Controllers; IT Lead acts as Processor; DPIAs for high‑risk processing.
  • Ownership & Access: Role‑based access with least privilege; quarterly permission reviews.
  • Security & Integrity: Encryption in transit/at rest; tested backups; no personal device storage without approval.
  • Retention & Minimisation: Keep only what’s needed; annual clean‑ups.
  • Ethical Use: Mission‑only use; no selling/sharing for marketing; clear privacy notices.

KPIs

  • Zero reportable GDPR breaches
  • 100% annual data protection training
  • Quarterly access reviews logged
  • Annual retention review completed

Pillar C – Security & Risk

We safeguard technology, data, and people by anticipating risks and applying strong controls.

Policy Commitments

  • Access: MFA on all accounts; least‑privilege permissions; quarterly account reviews; immediate leaver revocation.
  • Devices: Up‑to‑date patches and AV; personal devices require signed security agreement; only approved apps for data.
  • Risk: Maintain an IT Risk Register; quarterly Trustee review; mitigations for high‑impact risks.
  • Incidents: Prompt reporting; escalate to Trustees; GDPR reporting within 72 hours if required.
  • Continuity: Regular backups; annual disaster recovery test; backup cover for IT Lead absence.

KPIs

  • 100% MFA coverage
  • Quarterly risk & access reviews
  • Zero critical incidents without response
  • Successful annual restore test

Pillar D – Decision‑Making & Accountability

Decisions are transparent, inclusive, and traceable to avoid single‑person dependency.

Policy Commitments

  • Levels: Trustees = strategic; IT Lead = operational; volunteers/staff consulted.
  • Documentation: IT Decision Log with context, decision‑makers, options, rationale, review date.
  • Accountability: Trustees own governance; IT Lead enforces day‑to‑day; volunteers deliver; all users follow policy.
  • Escalation: Volunteer → IT Lead → Trustees; Trustees resolve priority conflicts.
  • Improvement: Quarterly governance reviews and feedback loop.

KPIs

  • 100% major decisions logged
  • Quarterly reviews completed
  • All disputes documented & resolved
  • Reduced single‑person dependency

Pillar E – Collaboration & Working Environment

We enable teamwork, knowledge sharing, and inclusive participation for everyone.

Policy Commitments

  • Tools: Microsoft Teams & SharePoint; email for formal notices; avoid unapproved tools.
  • Knowledge: SharePoint knowledge base; handovers; shared calendars/tasks.
  • Accessibility: Captions, screen readers; multi‑format training; inclusive participation.
  • Support: Clear support process and induction; peer help encouraged.
  • Feedback: Surveys, forums, and check‑ins; improvements align to all pillars.

KPIs

  • All IT processes documented
  • All volunteers complete induction
  • Accessibility features actively used
  • Fewer duplicate / unofficial tools

Ecosystem Map

A high‑level view of how our tools connect. (All access is role‑based with MFA.)

Core Platforms

  • Microsoft 365 tenant: Identity (Azure AD), Email, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive.
  • ERP (future‑ready): Central finance/data; integrates via secure connectors.
  • Power Platform: Low‑code apps and automations (approvals, onboarding).

Simple Flow

Users → Teams/Email → Requests (Forms/Approvals) → SharePoint/OneDrive (Docs)
                                        ↓
                                  ERP / Finance (data)
                                        ↓
                              Power Automate (notifications)

Rules & Guardrails

  • Least‑privilege access; quarterly reviews.
  • DPIAs for high‑risk processing; data retention schedule.
  • Approved tools only; change control for new apps.

Use Cases

1) New Volunteer Joiner

  1. Request submitted (Form) → approval by IT Lead.
  2. Account created with MFA; role‑based access applied.
  3. Induction: training links, Code of Conduct, knowledge base.
  4. Handover tasks set in shared planner; access reviewed in 90 days.

2) Subject Access Request (GDPR)

  1. Request logged in Teams/Helpdesk; acknowledge receipt.
  2. IT Lead triggers data search (SharePoint/Email/ERP).
  3. Review with Trustee (Data Controller); redact where required.
  4. Respond within statutory timelines; log decision & evidence.

Change Request (Lite)

  1. Describe need → assess against 5 pillars.
  2. Security & data check; DPIA if needed.
  3. Trial/pilot; decide; log; train; review after 8 weeks.

Support & SLA

How to get help and what to expect. (Times may change with volunteer capacity.)

How to get help

  • Use Teams channel #it‑support or the simple request form.
  • Emergency (security incident): tag IT Lead + a Trustee immediately.

Priorities & Targets

Priority Examples Target response Target resolution
P1 (Critical) Security incident, data loss, tenant outage 1 hour Same day
P2 (High) Access issues, broken workflow, ERP data sync 4 hours 2 business days
P3 (Normal) New user setup, permissions change, minor bugs 1 business day 5 business days
P4 (Low) How‑to questions, training, enhancements 3 business days As scheduled

Escalation

Volunteer → IT Lead → Named Trustee. For unresolved P1/P2 after targets, notify all Trustees.

Users → Volunteers → IT Lead → Trustees • We review IT every 3 months. Feedback is always welcome.
