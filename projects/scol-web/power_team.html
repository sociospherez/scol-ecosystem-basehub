Power Platform & Enterprise ERP Systems Expert

A senior technical and low-code delivery profile positioned to support automation, application development, reporting, workflow enablement and integration across ERP-centred environments including Microsoft Dynamics, SAP, ServiceNow, Workday, Oracle Financials and connected operational platforms.

Resource positioning

This profile is best positioned as a Power Platform and enterprise systems development resource, with strong capability across automation, workflow design, app development, data connectivity, integration support and ERP-aligned delivery.

The resource does not need to be presented as a pure Microsoft Dynamics specialist. The stronger positioning is broader: an ERP ecosystem enabler who can work across Microsoft, SAP, ServiceNow, Workday, Oracle and other business-critical platforms where process, data, automation and integration need to come together.
