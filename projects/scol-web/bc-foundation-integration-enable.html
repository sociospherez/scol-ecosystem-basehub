Why this conversation matters.

Not a heavy transformation workshop. A controlled starting point to understand what exists, what is needed, and what should be addressed first.

01 · Current state

Understand the setup

Review Business Central progress, existing configuration, current vendors, internal ownership and any open delivery concerns.

02 · Practical need

Clarify the ask

Identify whether support is needed for configuration, delivery coordination, integrations, HR workflows or operational support.

03 · Next step

Shape the right model

Keep the delivery structure lean, with the right mix of functional, coordination and technical support only where needed.

What kickoff looks like.

A lightweight session designed to create clarity before assigning people, budget or delivery activity.

01

Current landscape

What has already been set up, what is pending, and where delivery feels unclear.

02

Business priorities

Which operational areas matter first: HR, finance, approvals, contact centre, users or reporting.

03

Dependencies

What systems, data, people, decisions or integrations need to line up before moving forward.

04

Delivery shape

Agree whether a functional consultant is enough, or whether coordination and technical input are needed.

Simple resource model.

The aim is not to create a large team. It is to place the right capability around the right problem.

Functional

BC Functional Consultant

  • Maps business process
  • Configures Business Central
  • Supports HR/finance workflow alignment
  • Can coordinate smaller delivery activities
Coordination

Delivery Lead

  • Keeps priorities aligned
  • Tracks actions and dependencies
  • Supports business communication
  • Reduces delivery drift
As needed

Technical Support

  • Integrations and APIs
  • Customisation support
  • Automation workflows
  • System connectivity

Experience lens

Relevant prior pattern

Similar Microsoft ecosystem work has required aligning operational workflows, onboarding/offboarding, access governance, support processes, reporting, vendor coordination and integration points before scaling wider automation.

The same principle applies here: stabilise the foundation, then scale into automation, Copilot, AI assistants and continuous improvement.

The goal is not just to set up Business Central.

The goal is to create a connected operating foundation that supports people, process, finance, communication and future automation — without overwhelming the business at kickoff.
