Senior Technical Consultant & Integration Engineer

A delivery-focused technical and architectural resource profile positioned to support Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central implementation, cloud-enabled integration, data migration, testing, stabilisation and continuous improvement across enterprise operational environments.

Resource positioning

This profile is best positioned as a senior technical consultant supporting solution architecture, integration engineering, data migration and technical delivery within Business Central, HR, payroll and facilities management aligned ecosystems.

The resource complements functional and operational SMEs by focusing on the technical layer: environments, APIs, data flows, platform connectivity, testing, deployment support and ongoing improvement. This avoids duplicating the functional consultant role while strengthening implementation confidence and technical delivery capacity.
